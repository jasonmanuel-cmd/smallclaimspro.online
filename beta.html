Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Testing never requires paying, and you won't be asked to buy anything during the test.

Do I need a real small claims case?

No. Practice cases are best. Don't type in anything you wouldn't want stored.

What happens to my information?

Joining the group and opting in on Google Play doesn't send us your email through this page. Your group membership and test sign-up are handled by Google under Google's terms. If you use the app's case tools, what you type is handled like any SmallClaimsPro case. See our privacy policy.

How long does the test last?

At least 14 days. Please stay in until we email you that it's done.

Can I leave?

Yes, any time. Leave the Google Group, or open the Google Play test link again and choose the option to leave the test.
