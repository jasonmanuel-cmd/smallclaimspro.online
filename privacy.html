Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 · SmallClaimsPro · Bakersfield, CA

1. Who We Are

SmallClaimsPro is a product of Chaotically Organized AI, LLC, a Bakersfield, CA service-area business. Contact: support@smallclaimspro.online or (661) 683-0228.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you submit through our forms and tools, including:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Description of your dispute and case details
  • Company website if provided
  • Payment information (processed by Square — we do not store card numbers)

Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, device and browser type, and pages requested). We do not currently use third-party analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To deliver your action plan, demand letter, and court preparation documents
  • To respond to your questions and provide support
  • To send service-related communications about your submission
  • To improve our tools and content
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Ever.

4. Case Information and Confidentiality

The case details you submit are used to prepare your action plan and related documents. To do that, they are processed by the service providers listed in Section 5, which act on our behalf: your case details are sent to an AI provider to draft your documents, stored in our database, converted to PDF, and emailed to you. Submissions our system flags may be reviewed by our team. We do not share your case details with opposing parties or courts, and we do not file anything on your behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Third-Party Services

We use these providers to run the service. Each handles your information under its own terms and privacy policy.

  • Netlify: hosts this website and runs the code that receives your case submission. Keeps standard server logs.
  • Supabase: stores your case submission and its processing status.
  • OpenAI: receives your case details to draft your action plan and documents.
  • PDFShift: converts prepared documents to PDF.
  • Resend: sends emails to you, such as your action plan.
  • Formspree: receives messages sent through our contact form and delivers them to our email.
  • Square: processes payments. Card data is handled by Square; we do not store card numbers.
  • Google: if you join our Android app test, your tester group membership and test sign-up are handled by Google (Google Groups and Google Play) under Google's terms. Our tester page does not collect your email.

6. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of their information, and opt out of any sale of their information (we do not sell personal information). To exercise these rights, email support@smallclaimspro.online. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Our Android App

The SmallClaimsPro Android app opens this website inside the app. Information you enter in the app is collected, used and shared the same way as described in this policy.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information as long as necessary to fulfill your service request and for a reasonable period thereafter in case of follow-up questions. If you want your data deleted, contact us directly and we will process your request within 30 days.

9. Security

We implement reasonable measures to protect your information. Form submissions are transmitted via encrypted connections. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Contact
